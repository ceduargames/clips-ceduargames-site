Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how Clips CeduarGames handles information when a creator uses the desktop application.

Information the app processes

  • OAuth authorization tokens and basic account information returned by platforms the creator connects, such as a display name or account identifier.
  • Videos, captions, hashtags, privacy choices, interaction settings, and publishing times selected by the creator.
  • Local publication history and status information needed to show whether a requested upload succeeded.

How information is used

The app uses this information only to connect the creator's authorized accounts, display available publishing choices, upload creator-selected content, execute creator-requested local schedules, refresh authorization when permitted, and report publication status.

Storage and sharing

Clips CeduarGames does not operate a central server that stores creators' videos or social credentials. Credentials and tokens are stored on the creator's Windows computer and protected with Windows user-level encryption. Videos and publishing metadata are sent to a social platform only after the creator requests or schedules that publication. Information is shared with the selected platform as required to perform that request and is then governed by that platform's privacy policy.

Optional local metadata generation

If the creator enables the optional LM Studio integration, selected frames and prompts are processed by the creator's locally configured LM Studio server to suggest publishing text. This feature is optional, and the creator reviews the result before publication.

Retention and deletion

Local credentials, tokens, schedules, and history remain on the computer until the creator disconnects an account, deletes the local application data, or uninstalls the application. A creator can also revoke access from the connected platform's account settings. Platform-side copies of published content and authorization records are controlled through that platform.

Security

The app uses official authorization flows and does not ask for a creator's social-network password. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the project limits processing to what is needed for its publishing features.

Children

The application is intended for creators who are legally permitted to manage the connected social accounts. It is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Questions and privacy requests can be submitted through the project's public contact page.
