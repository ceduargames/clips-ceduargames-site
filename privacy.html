CLIPS CEDUARGAMES

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clips CeduarGames, an independent software project operated under the CeduarGames name ("Clips CeduarGames," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you use the Clips CeduarGames Windows desktop application (the "Application") or this public website (the "Website"). It also explains the choices available to you.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to the Application and Website. It does not replace the privacy policies of TikTok, YouTube, Instagram/Meta, GitHub, or any other third-party service you choose to use. Those services process information under their own policies and account settings.

2. Information handled by the Application

2.1 Files and content you select

The Application processes gameplay recordings, generated clips, audio, optional transcripts, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, hashtags, game information, and other context you provide. This information is used to analyze footage, create or combine clips, generate suggested text, and prepare posts.

2.2 Connected-account information

When you connect a social account through an official OAuth authorization flow, the Application may receive an access token, refresh token, token expiration time, platform account identifier, and display name. For TikTok, the Application requests user.info.basic to identify the connected account and video.publish to publish a video you expressly approve.

2.3 Publishing information

The Application handles the destination platform, local file path, caption and hashtags, audience or privacy selection, interaction choices, commercial-content disclosures, requested publication time, platform publication identifiers, processing status, and error messages.

2.4 Technical information

The Application may create local configuration, diagnostic messages, and history records needed to operate, troubleshoot, and display the status of a job. We do not use these records for advertising or cross-service tracking.

3. How information is obtained

Information comes directly from you when you select media, enter text, configure a post, or request account authorization; from your computer when local processing reads the files you selected; and from a connected platform when its official API returns authorized account information, publishing options, status, or errors.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We handle information only as needed to:

  • provide the features you request, including clip generation, local analysis, account connection, scheduling, publishing, and status checks;
  • authenticate your authorized social account and display which account is connected;
  • protect the Application, prevent unauthorized use, and diagnose failures;
  • comply with platform rules and applicable legal obligations; and
  • respond to support, privacy, or deletion requests you initiate.

Depending on applicable law, these activities rely on performance of the service you request, your consent or instructions, our legitimate interest in operating and securing the Application, and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw an authorization at any time, although this does not affect processing already completed.

5. Local processing and storage

The Application is designed primarily for local use. Selected source media, generated clips, optional transcripts, local AI results, settings, schedules, and publication history remain on your Windows computer unless you choose to send content to a connected service. OAuth tokens and configured credentials are stored locally and protected for the current Windows user using Windows data-protection facilities.

You control access to the computer and its files. Use a secured Windows account, keep the operating system updated, and protect local backups. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

6. When information is shared

The Application sends information to a social platform only when needed for a connection, option request, upload, publication, or status check that you initiate or schedule. For a TikTok direct post, this may include the video file, caption, privacy selection, interaction settings, and commercial-content disclosures. The receiving platform processes that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Optional locally configured AI or transcription components process the selected content on your computer. If you configure a third-party model provider or external endpoint yourself, information sent to that provider is governed by your configuration and the provider's terms.

We do not sell personal information, rent it, use it for behavioral advertising, or share it with data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a legitimate transfer of the project, subject to applicable safeguards.

7. Website data

This Website is a static informational site hosted through GitHub Pages. It does not provide user accounts, accept media uploads, or include our own advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub may receive standard network information such as IP address, browser data, and request logs when it serves the pages, according to GitHub's own privacy documentation.

8. Retention

Application data remains on your computer until you delete it, disconnect an account, clear the Application's local data, or uninstall the Application. Access tokens remain until they expire, are replaced, you disconnect, or access is revoked. Local schedule and history records remain until removed by you. A connected platform may retain uploaded or published content, logs, and account records according to its own retention rules even after you disconnect the Application.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, prevent abuse, keep necessary records, or meet legal obligations.

9. Your choices and rights

You can choose which files to process, whether to connect a platform, which account to use, and whether to publish. You can disconnect inside the Application and revoke access in the platform's security settings. You can also delete locally stored clips, schedules, history, tokens, and configuration.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to withdraw consent. Because most Application data is stored only on your computer, the fastest way to exercise these choices is through the Application, Windows file controls, and the connected platform. See our Data Deletion Instructions for specific steps.

10. International processing

Social platforms and the Website host may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies describe their locations and transfer safeguards. The Application itself does not operate a central media-storage server.

11. Children

The Application is intended for creators who are legally able to authorize connected accounts and publish content. It is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by applicable law and the connected platform. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the Website.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Application, platform requirements, or applicable law changes. The current version and effective date will remain available at this URL. Material changes will be presented through the Website or Application when reasonably appropriate.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy, this Policy, or a deletion request can be submitted through the Clips CeduarGames Support page. Do not include passwords, access tokens, client secrets, or sensitive personal information in a public support request.
