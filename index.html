Desktop publishing workflow for gaming creators

Create, review, schedule, and publish gaming clips.

Clips CeduarGames helps creators turn their own recordings into short-form clips and publish them to social accounts they explicitly authorize.

View the project

Creator-controlled access

Each platform is connected through its official authorization screen. The creator can disconnect an account at any time.

Review before publishing

The creator selects the clip, reviews its caption and hashtags, chooses the available privacy and interaction settings, and confirms the publication.

Local-first design

Clips, credentials, tokens, and scheduled jobs remain on the creator's Windows computer until the creator requests an upload.

How TikTok integration works

  1. The creator selects Connect TikTok and authorizes the requested permissions in TikTok's official Login Kit page.
  2. The app displays the posting choices TikTok makes available for the connected creator.
  3. The creator selects a local video, reviews the caption and settings, and explicitly chooses to publish now or schedule a local publication.
  4. At the chosen time, the app sends the video directly from the creator's computer to TikTok through the Content Posting API.
